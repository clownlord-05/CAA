# Ke Hoach Website Chi Hoi Quang Cao Thu Do (CAA)

Phien ban: 1.0  
Ngay lap: 15/09/2026  
Nguon dau vao: `plan.txt` va `Bao Cao Tong Hop Chi Hoi Quang Cao Thu Do (CAA) V2.txt`

## 1. Tom Tat Dinh Huong

Website CAA khong nen chi la mot trang gioi thieu. Dinh huong dung la mot cong thong tin chinh thuc ket hop nen tang hoi vien va mang luoi B2B cho cong dong doanh nghiep quang cao tai Ha Noi.

Muc tieu cua website:

1. Tao bo mat chinh thong, hien dai va dang tin cay cho Chi hoi Quang cao Thu do.
2. So hoa quy trinh dang ky, duyet va quan ly hoi vien.
3. Xay dung danh ba B2B giup hoi vien tim doi tac theo linh vuc dich vu.
4. Truyen thong su kien, phong trao, giai dau, hoi thao va hoat dong cong dong.
5. Cung cap kho tai lieu noi bo cho hoi vien da xac thuc.
6. Giam viec xu ly thu cong cho Ban thu ky thong qua CMS, form, QR check-in va phan quyen.

Dinh vi san pham: "Cong thong tin va mang luoi ket noi nganh quang cao Thu do."

## 2. Gia Dinh Va Nguyen Tac Thiet Ke

### 2.1 Gia Dinh

- Doi tuong chinh la doanh nghiep quang cao, agency, don vi OOH, in an, digital marketing, to chuc su kien, nha cung cap vat tu, doi tac truyen thong va hoi vien hien huu.
- Ban thu ky CAA can mot he thong de tu quan tri tin bai, su kien, hoi vien, tai lieu va dang ky.
- Du lieu trong bao cao ve moc thoi gian, lanh dao, hoi vien tieu bieu va su kien can duoc doi chieu voi nguon chinh thuc truoc khi dang cong khai.
- Website uu tien tieng Viet. Tieng Anh co the bo sung o giai doan sau neu CAA can ket noi doi tac quoc te.

### 2.2 Nguyen Tac

- Chinh thong nhung khong kho cung: can tao cam giac day la mot to chuc nghe nghiep co uy tin, nhung van nang dong va gan voi cong dong doanh nghiep.
- Uu tien mobile-first: nguoi dung la chu doanh nghiep, giam doc, hoi vien ban ron; thao tac phai nhanh tren dien thoai.
- Khong che so lieu: chi hien thi thong ke neu CAA cung cap du lieu chinh xac. Neu chua co, dung placeholder noi bo de xac minh.
- Tach ro cong khai va noi bo: thong tin lien he, tai lieu, danh ba day du chi danh cho tai khoan hoi vien da xac thuc.
- Admin phai de dung: CMS va quy trinh duyet phai phu hop voi Ban thu ky, khong phu thuoc lap trinh vien cho moi bai viet/su kien.

## 3. Nhom Nguoi Dung Va Quyen

### 3.1 Guest

Nguoi chua dang nhap.

Co the:

- Xem trang chu, gioi thieu, su menh, tam nhin, lich su hinh thanh.
- Xem tin tuc, hoat dong, su kien cong khai.
- Xem danh ba hoi vien ban rut gon.
- Tim hieu quyen loi hoi vien.
- Gui don dang ky gia nhap.
- Gui lien he/hop tac.

Khong the:

- Xem thong tin lien he noi bo cua hoi vien.
- Tai tai lieu chuyen nganh noi bo.
- Dung nut "Lien he doi tac" day du neu chua duoc xac thuc.

### 3.2 Member

Tai khoan hoi vien da duoc duyet.

Co the:

- Gui yeu cau ket noi doi tac.
- Dang ky tham du su kien.
- Nhan QR check-in qua email.
- Tai tai lieu noi bo neu duoc phan quyen.
- Xem lich su su kien, dang ky, tai lieu da tai.

### 3.3 Admin / Ban Thu Ky

Co the:

- Quan ly bai viet, banner, media, album, video.
- Tao va sua su kien.
- Quan ly dang ky su kien, xuat danh sach, quet/check-in QR.
- Duyet ho so hoi vien moi.
- Khoa/mo tai khoan hoi vien.

### 3.4 Super Admin

Quyen cao nhat.

Co the:

- Quan ly admin khac.
- Cau hinh he thong, email, domain, backup, tich hop.
- Xem audit log.
- Thay doi vai tro va quyen truy cap.

### 3.5 Event Staff

Tai khoan phu cho ngay dien ra su kien.

Co the:

- Xem danh sach khach da dang ky mot su kien duoc gan.
- Quet QR check-in.
- Cap nhat trang thai co mat/vang mat.

## 8. Huong Thiet Ke UI/UX

### 8.1 Tinh Cach Thuong Hieu

Tu khoa:

- Chinh thong.
- Ket noi.
- Nang dong.
- Doanh nghiep.
- Thu do.
- Tien phong chuyen doi so.

Website nen tranh cam giac "landing page mau". Cau truc nen mang dang cong thong tin nghe nghiep: nhieu thong tin, de quet nhanh, ro chuc nang, co tin tuc va lich su kien song dong.

### 8.2 Phong Cach Hinh Anh

Uu tien anh that:

- Dai hoi.
- Gala.
- Hoi thao.
- Giai Pickleball.
- Anh lanh dao.
- Anh doanh nghiep hoi vien.

Khong nen dung anh stock toi mau, mo nhat, khong lien quan. Neu thieu anh, dung layout editorial va khoi thong tin thay vi chen anh gia.

### 8.3 Mau Sac Goi Y

Can bam theo logo chinh thuc cua CAA neu co. Neu chua co brand guideline, co the dung huong:

- Mau chu dao: do tham/do thu do tao cam giac uy tin va nang luong.

- Mau phu: vang kim tiet che cho diem nhan nghi thuc.
- Mau cong nghe: xanh duong/lam dam dung it cho lien ket, dashboard va trang thai.

Khong nen de website bi mot mau do toan bo. Dung mau chu dao lam diem nhan, con lai phai co nhieu khoang tho va nen trung tinh.

Can ho tro tieng Viet tot.

Goi y:

## 9. Kien Truc Ky Thuat De Xuat

### 9.1 Stack Khuyen Nghi

Phuong an nen dung: web app tuy bien, khong chi la CMS co san.

De xuat:

- Frontend: Next.js/React hoac framework SSR tuong duong.
- Backend/API: Node.js/NestJS, Laravel hoac Next.js API tuy doi ngu.
- Database: PostgreSQL.
- ORM: Prisma/Drizzle hoac ORM tuong duong.
- Storage: S3-compatible storage cho anh, video, PDF.
- Cache/queue: Redis va job queue cho email, OCR, nen anh.
- Auth: email/password, magic link hoac SSO sau nay.
- Email: SMTP/domain email chinh thuc.
- Search: PostgreSQL full-text cho MVP, Meilisearch/Typesense khi danh ba va noi dung lon.
- Deployment: VPS/cloud co CDN, backup tu dong va SSL.

Ly do:

- Can phan quyen hoi vien, admin, tai lieu noi bo.
- Can danh ba B2B va ho so doanh nghiep tuy bien.
- Can QR check-in va workflow su kien.
- Can xu ly media/anh scan rieng.
- CMS don gian nhu WordPress co the nhanh luc dau nhung se kho sach se khi them portal hoi vien va quyen noi bo.

### 9.2 So Do He Thong

Thanh phan:

- Public Website.
- Member Portal.
- Admin CMS.
- API Server.
- Database.
- File Storage.
- Background Worker.
- Email Service.
- OCR/Image Processing Service.
- Analytics.

Luong xu ly anh:

1. Admin upload anh.
2. File vao storage tam.
3. Worker kiem tra kich thuoc, nen anh, tao thumbnail.
4. Neu anh co text, OCR/orientation detector xac dinh huong chu.
5. Anh bi xoay duoc auto-rotate.
6. File sau xu ly duoc luu ban final va gan vao MediaAsset.

Luong QR:

1. User dang ky su kien.
2. He thong tao qr_token duy nhat.
3. Email gui QR.
4. Event Staff quet QR.
5. API xac thuc token, quyen su kien va trang thai.
6. Ghi nhan check-in va audit log.

## 16. Rủi Ro Va Cach Xu Ly

### Du Lieu Chua Xac Minh

- Yeu cau CAA duyet noi dung truoc launch.
- Khong publish thong tin lien he noi bo neu chua co dong y.

### Feature Creep

Rui ro: lam qua nhieu tinh nang tu dau khien cham launch.

Cach xu ly:

- Chia MVP va V2 ro rang.
- QR/OCR/CRM/thanh toan co the lam sau neu ngan sach hoac timeline han che.

### Chat Luong Hinh Anh

Rui ro: thieu anh that, website trong nhu template.

Cach xu ly:

- Thu thap anh su kien that.
- Neu anh chua du, dung bo cuc editorial va typography thay vi anh stock.
- Quy dinh kich thuoc va ti le anh cho admin.

### Bao Mat Tai Lieu Noi Bo

Rui ro: file noi bo bi chia se cong khai.

Cach xu ly:

- Dung signed URL hoac route tai file qua backend co auth.
- Watermark PDF cho tai lieu quan trong.
- Audit log download.

### Check-in Tai Su Kien

Rui ro: mat mang tai dia diem, QR khong quet duoc.

Cach xu ly:

- Co tim kiem thu cong theo ten/sdt/email.
- Co che offline tam thoi neu can.
- In danh sach backup truoc su kien quan trong.

## 18. Cau Hoi Can Chot Truoc Khi Lam

1. CAA da co logo va brand guideline chinh thuc chua?

4. Co can thu phi hoi vien/su kien online trong MVP khong?
5. QR check-in co bat buoc trong ban dau khong hay day sang V2?

## 20. Ket Luan

Website CAA nen duoc xay nhu mot he thong hoi vien va ket noi nganh, khong phai mot website tin tuc don thuan. Trong ban dau, can tap trung vao bon tru cot: uy tin to chuc, dang ky hoi vien, danh ba B2B va su kien. Khi bon tru cot nay chay on dinh, cac tinh nang nang cao nhu QR check-in, OCR, thanh toan va CRM noi bo se co nen tang tot de phat trien tiep.
